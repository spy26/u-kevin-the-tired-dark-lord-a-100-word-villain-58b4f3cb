Sign here, hero. And here. The moonlight tax is two coppers a beam, arrears back to the third age — I conquered the kingdom with paperwork, and paperwork, unlike swords, never sleeps. Neither, apparently, do I. Where is my seal? It was here. It is always here. Ah — in my other hand. The scheme is simple: own the night, rent it back by the hour, retire to a cottage with... with what? I had it a moment ago. A garden. Yes. A garden, and no more forms. Sign, then. Or don't. The moon has already paid, and frankly, so have I.
